# Proposal

## Redesign of 2026-11-12

On 2026-11-12 the operator agreed a full-factorial redesign of Experiment 35 (pre-freeze amendment A1; change `experiment-35-full-document-rag-benchmark`, design D0; `protocol.md` section 0). This change now prepares the harness and OMRS for that design:

- Four datasets (FinanceBench 150, FreshStack 203, BioASQ 707, Legal RAG Bench 100; 1,160 questions).
- Search stage: embedder (Qwen3-Embedding 0.6B, 4B, 8B) x chunk size (512, 1,024, 2,048 tokens, 15% overlap) x reranker (off, Qwen3-Reranker 0.6B, 4B, 8B) x hybrid (off, BM25 on). 9 indexes and 72 search setups per dataset, run once each. Setups are named by their settings.
- Answer stage: 12 setups (chunk size x reranker on or off x hybrid on or off) at the embedder and reranker sizes chosen by a pre-registered rule, 3 runs each: 41,760 answers.
- Claim checkers GLM-5.3 (main) and Clef-Flash (second). Granite Guardian and hypothesis H-CC are dropped. The refusal rule and its classifier gate stay.
- The $250 cap is replaced by an estimate of about $650 and a stop rule at a $975 ceiling.

Older text in this change that names six arms, `documents-profile-4b`, `chunking-off`, Granite Guardian, H-CC or the $250 cap was rewritten on 2026-11-12. The earlier plan stays readable in git history and in the dated design notes.

## Why

The Experiment 35 main run cannot start at the protocol freeze (gate G7) as the harness stands. The harness sends every hosted call one at a time, so the paid answer and judge calls of 12 answer setups, four datasets and three runs take too long. A rate-limit reply is saved as an answer error and is never sent again. Ragas runs only in a pre-freeze script. Three runs in a row on one pod take three times the pod time of one run. The refusal sentences in answers change the faithfulness figures, and the protocol has no rule for them yet.

The redesign adds three more gaps. OMRS has no way to run Qwen3-Reranker: its reranker backends are an ONNX cross-encoder and a sentence-transformers cross-encoder, and the experiment needs the reranker on the same llama.cpp runtime as the embedders. The harness knows only the six one-factor arms and sets no chunk size. And it cannot load Legal RAG Bench or score a FinanceBench answer's numbers.

Two problems sit in the OMRS app, not in the harness. A rate-limited answer comes back as a plain generation error, so a caller can find the rate limit only by reading the error text. A native full-text search writes to the store on a read. The operator decided (2026-11-12) to fix both in the app, in this change, before the freeze, so the benchmark measures the shipped app and not a harness workaround.

## What Changes

- App fix A (design D25): a rate-limited answer becomes a typed error. The result keeps status `error` and `failure_stage` `generation`, and gains a `rate_limited` record (`kind`, `http_status`, `retry_after_seconds`). An HTTP 402 with no `Retry-After` is its own kind, `out_of_credit`: it is not retried, and the message says to top up the account. OMRS sends an HTTP 402 again after a `Retry-After` of 30 s or less; HTTP 429 keeps the client libraries' own retries. A rate-limited claim judge keeps the answer and adds a `verification_rate_limited` record of the same shape. The web chat shows a line per kind. The OpenAPI contract declares both records.
- App fix B (design D26): a native full-text query only reads the store. The ingest builds or refreshes the full-text index at its end when the resolved sparse backend is `native`. A query on a collection with no index falls back to BM25 with a warning that names the ingest remedy.
- App addition C (design D27): a llama.cpp reranker backend. One new file `src/omrs/core/retrieval/reranker_llamacpp.py` sends query-document pairs to the local router's `/v1/rerank` endpoint for a model whose preset sets `pooling: rank`. One `register()` line, one backend map entry and the accepted value `llamacpp` for `retrieval.rerank_backend`. Scores are normalised to (0, 1) like the other backends.
- Harness setups (design D28): the six arms become 72 search setups and 12 answer setups named by their settings (`e<embedder>-c<chunk size>-r<reranker>-h<hybrid>`). Every process writes `chunking.chunk_size`, `chunking.markdown_chunk_size` and `chunking.chunk_overlap` for its chunk-size level, `retrieval.rerank_backend: llamacpp` and the reranker model, and `retrieval.hybrid_sparse_backend: bm25` for hybrid-on setups. The search stage needs the embedder and the reranker loaded together on the router.
- Datasets (design D29): a Legal RAG Bench loader, the 203-query FreshStack set, and a FinanceBench number-match check.
- Operator decisions of 2026-11-13: the FreshStack corpus drops the continuity documents and the overlay runbook; BioASQ and Legal passages are written as `.md`, so all four datasets count Qwen3 tokens (test-checked); the Ragas stage adds `AnswerCorrectness` (Ragas 0.4.3) against reference answers on all four datasets, checked against 100 operator labels by Cohen's kappa (design D33); every answer is one round (`answer.context_window` 131,072, `answer.max_rounds` 1); each search setup runs once (design D34). Budget: about $650, ceiling $975.
- Methods-review fixes of 2026-11-13 (pre-freeze amendment A2; design D35; task group 15): unit-level scoring on the top 100 chunks collapsed to 10 distinct units; token-matched answer setups (512 x 40, 1,024 x 20; 14 answer setups); a closed-book run; split-half selection; one `statsmodels` mixed model per dataset (new experiment-only dependency, approved); two labellers with 400 L1 items and a 30% L2 overlap; returned model and provider per hosted call; reranker Spearman check before pinning; MDE from data. Budget: about $650, ceiling $975.
- Answer context budget (design D30): a test proves that the largest evidence set of every answer setup synthesises without the earlier context overflow, and each answer row records its rounds, evidence characters and truncation.
- Selection rule and factorial statistics (design D31): a script applies the pre-registered embedder and reranker selection rule to saved search scores, and the analysis computes the marginal contrasts of H1 to H8 with the existing statistics functions.
- The harness reads the `rate_limited` record instead of parsing error text, and stops at once on `out_of_credit`. Every hybrid-on setup pins `retrieval.hybrid_sparse_backend: bm25`, as the protocol defines it.
- The existing `hybrid-retrieval` wording on unindexed rows is corrected to match the contract tests. The seal step creates no full-text index; its fingerprint stays as a check that no replicate stage writes the shared store.
- Add a per-provider concurrency limit for hosted calls: answers (OpenRouter), the `chat` claim judge (GLM-5.3 on Z.AI), the Ragas judge and the claim classifier (OpenRouter). Each limit is a command-line setting with a frozen default. The run record holds the values used. The local judge (`clef_flash`) stays at one call at a time.
- Retry a rate-limit reply (HTTP 429 or 402) after the `Retry-After` time, or after a bounded backoff with jitter. Never save a rate-limit error as an answer, verdict or score. After a bounded number of attempts, stop the stage cleanly so that `--resume` continues.
- Write rows in frozen question order, so a parallel run and a serial run give the same files. Keep each finished result on disk at once, so a stop never repeats a paid call.
- Keep retrieval serial in the answer stage. Only the hosted answer call runs in parallel.
- Add a `ragas` stage to the main harness. It uses the pre-freeze judge, metrics and settings. Each replicate pod runs it on its own run folder right after its answer and judge stages, with `answer_relevancy` embeddings from the pod's local router and the pinned 4B GGUF.
- Run every experiment stage in one environment: the standard RTX 4090 pod (same image, llama.cpp build and GGUF files). This includes hosted-only stages such as the claim classifier. The image is `runpod/base:1.6.0-cuda1281-ubuntu2404`, pinned by digest at the freeze; the Mac pod-creation script passes that one value as the pod image and as `EXP35_POD_IMAGE`. Each stage records its environment and refuses to start on a difference; the join fails a run whose record differs. Only steps that read saved files and write checks, statistics or reports run on the Mac.
- Keep each Ragas answer's call records apart when answers are scored at once, and never save a rate limit as a Ragas metric error. The pre-freeze code shares one recording seam and saves any metric exception as an error.
- Split the run across pods. One build pod builds the 9 indexes of each dataset once on a RunPod network volume, seals them, and runs the 72 search setups. After the selection rule, three replicate pods run `--run 1`, `--run 2` and `--run 3` of the 12 answer setups at the same time. Each pod reads the shared index without a write, and writes only its own `run<n>/` folders. Router state and scratch settings go to a pod-local folder. Each pod keeps its own run records.
- Add a `join` step. It checks that all three runs are complete for every answer setup and dataset, and that the shared index did not change. Determinism and statistics refuse to start without a passing join. The determinism report names the machine of each run.
- Add the rebuild comparison (gate G2, Exp 35 task 4.2) to this change. The build pod rebuilds the `e4b-c1024` index of each dataset in a fresh process and compares its retrieval with the shared index, question by question. A failed check is a fixed stop: the replicate pods do not start until the cause is fixed and the indexes are built again. The join needs a passing rebuild report.
- Add a named-question smoke run (`--qids`). The build pod uses it for the zero-error recheck of FreshStack questions `77536364` and `77762811` in every answer setup, before the replicate pods start.
- Sort each claim sentence into factual claim, refusal or mixed with a frozen classifier: a fixed-prompt LLM, `openai/gpt-oss-120b`, pinned (operator decision, 2026-11-12). It runs as a `claimkinds` stage on each replicate pod. Mixed sentences and negative findings that answer the question count as factual claims. Primary faithfulness uses factual claims only. The rule first served H-CC (registered 2026-11-11); since the 2026-11-12 redesign dropped H-CC, the rule stands on its own.
- Report refusals apart: the refusal rate per answer setup and dataset (a primary descriptive outcome, because a high rate can show a retrieval failure) and each checker's verdicts on refusals. Faithfulness on all claims, and per-checker bounds, become secondary robustness results.
- Gate the classifier before the freeze: operator hand labels on a seeded sample of about 100 smoke claims from the three smoke datasets, with pass thresholds fixed before labelling (approved: refusal-against-not agreement of at least 0.90 and refusal recall of at least 0.90, weighted, seed 20261112). Add a small labelling page for the operator.
- Add one tested `cohen_kappa` function to the shared statistics code (approved 2026-11-12). It gives the GLM-5.3 against Clef-Flash kappa.
- Run one pre-freeze test pod session on the standard 4090 pod with the pinned image, after the code is merged and tested (about 6 h, about $5 of pod time plus a few dollars of hosted calls). It measures the concurrency values and the costs; Qwen3-Reranker throughput at each size; the VRAM of the 8B embedder and the 8B reranker loaded together; and the index build time per chunk size. It checks each reranker GGUF file, runs the first budget check, rechecks FreshStack questions `77536364` and `77762811` with zero errors, and runs every new stage end to end once on its own small indexes. The frozen protocol holds the measured values. The operator gives the go after a price quote, and the pod is deleted when done.
- Write the run procedure, the measured values and the claim-kind rule into `protocol.md`. Write the budget into `protocol.md` (section 0.8, replacing Exp 35 task 1.11): an estimate of about $650 and a ceiling of $975 for pods and hosted calls together, checked before the freeze, on the build pod after the search stage, and while the replicate pods run.

Why factual claims only: faithfulness is meant to measure whether checked facts are backed by the sources. A refusal is a statement about retrieval, not a fact from a source. The checkers also grade refusals very differently, so with refusals in, faithfulness would partly measure refusal grading.

Evidence (smoke data, 5 questions per dataset, direction only): refusal claims are 15 of 278 on BioASQ (5.4%), 33 of 143 on FinanceBench (23.1%) and 20 of 383 on the FreshStack rerun (5.2%). The 76% figure from the invalid first FreshStack smoke run is not evidence. On refusal claims, Granite Guardian (since dropped) said unsupported on 67% to 94%, with a kappa of about 0 against GLM-5.3 (0.02 to 0.07). GLM-5.3 says unsupported on 6.7% to 15%, Clef-Flash on 20% to 21%. The pre-freeze regular expression fails as a classifier: about 9 of 15 sentences it flags are answers or mixed, and it misses some refusals.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `benchmark-replicate-runs`: one environment for every stage, building the indexes once, checking a rebuild of the reference index, running the repetitions of a benchmark on separate machines against one shared read-only index, and joining the runs before determinism and statistics.
- `benchmark-answer-scoring`: answer-level scoring of a benchmark run: parallel answers with serial retrieval, the Ragas stage, the claim-kind classifier with its gate and its refusal reporting, and the answer context record.

### Modified Capabilities

- `benchmark-harness`: adds requirements for per-provider concurrency of hosted calls, rate-limit retry and clean stop, row files that are the same for parallel and serial runs, setups named by settings with pinned chunk, reranker and sparse settings, and the answer-stage selection rule.
- `grounded-answer-synthesis`: adds a typed `rate_limited` record for a rate-limited generation, the `out_of_credit` kind, the short HTTP 402 retry, and a typed `verification_rate_limited` record for a rate-limited claim judge.
- `answer-sampling`: the answer contract declares `rate_limited` and `verification_rate_limited`.
- `hybrid-retrieval`: the full-text index is built and refreshed at ingest, never by a query; a native query never writes to the store; the wording on unindexed rows matches the contract tests.
- `reranker-backend-selection`: adds the `llamacpp` backend, which scores through the local llama.cpp router.

These capabilities add to the requirements that the open change `experiment-35-full-document-rag-benchmark` proposes under `evaluation-observability` and `document-rag-evaluation`. No requirement name is shared.
